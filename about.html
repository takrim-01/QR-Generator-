About QRCraft

QRCraft is a simple web-based tool that lets you generate and scan QR codes instantly. It is designed to remove complexity and provide a fast, clean, and reliable experience without requiring any installation or login.

What this project is

QRCraft is built as an all-in-one QR code generator and scanner that runs directly in the browser. It allows users to create QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi details, email content, and contact information, and also scan QR codes using a camera or image upload.

What you can do with it

  • Generate QR codes for URLs, text, Wi-Fi, email, and contacts
  • Scan QR codes using your device camera
  • Upload images to decode QR codes
  • Customize QR appearance such as color and size
  • Download generated QR codes in image format

Why it exists

Most QR tools are either overloaded with ads, require sign-up, or are slow to use. QRCraft was created to solve that problem by offering a lightweight, fast, and distraction-free alternative.

Privacy

No data is stored on servers. All QR generation and scanning happens directly in your browser. Nothing is tracked or saved.

Technology

This project is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with open-source QR libraries for generation and scanning.

Who it is for

QRCraft is designed for students, developers, small businesses, and anyone who needs a quick and reliable QR solution without complexity.
