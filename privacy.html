Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how QRCraft handles your information. The goal is simple: to provide QR tools that work without collecting or storing your personal data.

Data Collection

QRCraft does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All QR code generation and scanning processes happen directly in your browser.

How the tool works

When you generate or scan a QR code, the processing happens locally on your device. No content is sent to any server, and no history is saved.

Camera and file access

The QR scanner may request access to your camera or uploaded images. This access is used only for scanning purposes and is never recorded or stored.

Cookies and tracking

QRCraft does not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising. If any cookies are used, they are strictly for basic site functionality only.

Third-party libraries

This website uses open-source QR libraries for generating and scanning QR codes. These libraries run locally in the browser and do not collect user data.

Data security

Since no data is stored or transmitted, there is no risk of data leakage from QRCraft’s systems.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can contact us anytime.
